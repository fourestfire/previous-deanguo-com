art shopper: an e-commerce website for bob ross paintings (2017)

context

Along with three other Fullstack Academy developers, I developed a e-commerce website dedicated to selling Bob Ross paintings. Specifically, on the backend I worked on setting up our routing, and on the frontend, I built our home and product pages, with an eye towards ensuring our website was mobile responsive.
